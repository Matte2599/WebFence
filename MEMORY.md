### Decisioni tecniche da conservare

- Il laboratorio M0 di 10.000 fixture resta separato dalla scansione. La [alpha tecnica M1](DOCS/it/M1-VALIDATION.md) collega progetto dichiarato/autorizzato, run gestita, broker HTTP(S) controllato, discovery HTML osservativa, controllo `HTTP-XCTO-001`, ledger redatto e GUI IT/EN. È un primo controllo di header, non un audit generale. Nessun LLM è richiesto. La GUI M1 supporta una origine e un seed per scansione; il core ne supporta più entro limiti espliciti.
- Il primo [blocco core M2](DOCS/it/M2-INTELLIGENCE-CACHE.md) aggiunge cache SQLite CVE/NVD separata, sync NVD transazionale per finestre esplicite e recupero CVE per ID, con hash/provenienza e stato di aggiornamento. Le prove locali sintetiche di paginazione, rollback e riapertura sono superate; non è stata fatta una sincronizzazione reale né un'importazione di catalogo completo.
- Il [matching core M2](DOCS/it/M2-MATCHING.md) richiede segnali prodotto/versione espliciti e mantiene `unknown` per condizioni o schemi non supportati; non deriva CVE dagli header M1. Backport e verifica richiedono attestazioni esplicite, non prove automatiche. Le fixture sintetiche e le regressioni locali sono superate; non esiste ancora una misura di accuratezza su corpus reale.
- La [simulazione extra M2](DOCS/it/M2-ACCURACY-SIMULATION.md) misura solo 30 casi sintetici: 17 conclusioni corrette su 20 casi con verità nota, tre astensioni e dieci casi indeterminati. Per NVD senza CPE completo serve ora la parte `a`/`o`/`h`; qualificatori CVE non dimostrati e versioni discordanti restano `unknown`. Non è una misura di accuratezza reale.
- Il [core report M2](DOCS/it/M2-REPORTS.md) esporta una run conclusa in JSON/HTML IT/EN con manifest JCS, JWS Ed25519 e verifica offline a fiducia esterna. La firma usa una chiave dell'operatore nel portachiavi nativo; rotazione e revoca agiscono sul registro pubblico locale. Le prove sintetiche di alterazione e regressione locale sono superate, ma la fiducia offline non garantisce revoca corrente né orario attestato.
- Il [desktop M2 esteso](DOCS/it/M2-GUI-EXTENSION.md) espone aggiornamento manuale NVD/CVE, parte/CPE completo, backport e verifica attestati, stato di entrambe le fonti, export/verifica e gestione locale delle chiavi pubbliche. Le attestazioni nel form si azzerano quando cambia segnale/CVE/run; le correlazioni GUI sono effimere fino all'export. La chiave privata resta nel portachiavi; le regressioni Qt con portachiavi sintetico e la resa Cocoa/offscreen IT/EN sono state verificate localmente. Non derivare CVE dagli header M1 e non fare download feed all'avvio.
- Il [primo blocco core M3](DOCS/it/M3-BROWSER-GATE.md) aggiunge un gate per richieste browser legato a run gestita: scope/percorso, tipi ammessi, budget, concorrenza e revoca. Non esiste ancora un adattatore browser; worker e WebSocket sono negati. Un interceptor non prova contenimento dell'egress o limiti di processo.
- Il [secondo blocco core M3](DOCS/it/M3-BROWSER-PROXY.md) aggiunge un proxy HTTP anonimo su loopback con credenziale effimera, che passa dal gate e dal broker con IP fissati ([ADR-009](DOCS/it/ADR-009-BROWSER-HTTP-BOUNDARY.md)). Le prove sintetiche `httptest` e le regressioni locali sono passate; HTTPS CONNECT resta negato. Un successivo adattatore opzionale lega una sola sessione di prova, senza inoltrare cookie del browser. Il proxy non forza un processo esterno a usarlo.
- Il [laboratorio browser M3](DOCS/it/M3-BROWSER-LAB.md) usa Qt WebEngine/MIQT con profilo effimero, interceptor e proxy su fixture locali ([ADR-010](DOCS/it/ADR-010-QT-BROWSER-LAB.md)). La prova locale macOS ha superato documento, script, `fetch`, subresource e redirect esclusi; il laboratorio passa nei job CI macOS e Linux del commit `b82693a`. Non è una funzionalità desktop o un isolamento di processo dimostrato.
- Il [quarto blocco sperimentale M3](DOCS/it/ADR-011-BROWSER-HELPER.md) sposta Qt in un helper separato: configurazione e credenziale proxy su pipe solo dopo il confine di processo, ambiente e directory temporanea ridotti, durata/output limitati e terminazione dei discendenti. I test sintetici e il laboratorio Qt locale sono passati; la CI del branch/merge va valutata per commit. Il Job Object Windows è stato compilato localmente ma non eseguito qui; egress indipendente e quote aggregate restano assenti.
- Il [blocco risorse Unix M3](DOCS/it/M3-BROWSER-RESOURCE-LIMITS.md) applica nell'helper, prima di Qt, limiti rigidi per descrittori, dimensione file e core dump su macOS/Linux. Il test sintetico verifica eredità nel discendente e limiti del genitore invariati; prova Qt locale macOS e regressioni Go sono passate. macOS ha rifiutato `RLIMIT_AS`; il primo tentativo Linux con 16 GiB ha caricato il documento senza eseguire lo script. Il limite di spazio virtuale è stato rimosso per la possibile interferenza con le grandi riserve V8; la causa precisa non è provata. Mancano limiti di memoria/processi aggregati ed egress indipendente.
- La [prova IPC M3](DOCS/it/M3-BROWSER-UNIX-SCHEME.md) usa un proxy autenticato su socket Unix e uno schema Qt per documento, script e `fetch` sintetici su macOS ([ADR-013](DOCS/it/ADR-013-BROWSER-UNIX-SCHEME.md)). I test locali del proxy, la suite Go e il laboratorio Qt sono passati. Lo schema non riproduce status/header HTTP né l'origine reale. Non è integrato nel desktop né chiude il criterio browser. Il job Linux con Qt 6.4 non può provare `fetch` su questo schema, che richiede `FetchApiAllowed` da Qt 6.6.
- Il [filtro Linux M3](DOCS/it/M3-BROWSER-LINUX-NETWORK.md) applica seccomp `TSYNC` nel secondo helper prima di Qt. La prima versione negava nuovi socket INET; la [revisione ADR-014](DOCS/it/ADR-014-LINUX-BROWSER-NETWORK.md) passa quattro connessioni al broker preaperte dal genitore e nega anche nuovi `connect` Unix, socket datagram e `sendto` con destinazione, mantenendo IPC Qt sui socket già connessi. Test Linux ARM64 in container, laboratorio Qt Linux locale e relativo job CI passano; il primo helper HTTP resta senza filtro e il browser non è nel desktop. Quote aggregate, semantica HTTP(S) e altre piattaforme restano aperte.
- La [fattibilità M3 per piattaforma](DOCS/it/M3-BROWSER-PLATFORM-FEASIBILITY.md) registra prove locali temporanee: un bundle App Sandbox senza rete nega TCP; Qt WebEngine 6.11.2 fallisce sulla registrazione Mach di Chromium anche con sandbox Chromium disabilitata; WebKit con schema sintetico passa senza sandbox e con permesso di rete, ma nel bundle senza permesso non carica la pagina. Sono osservazioni locali, non prove di impossibilità generale. Windows Qt WebEngine non compila con MinGW. L'[ADR-015](DOCS/it/ADR-015-BROWSER-PLATFORM-BOUNDARY.md) propone di mantenere il browser fuori dalla GUI e il primo criterio M3 aperto finché non esista un confine OS e un runtime verificato per piattaforma; non modifica la scelta dell'autore Go/Qt Widgets per il desktop.
- La [prova Linux Chromium/CDP](DOCS/it/M3-BROWSER-CDP-LAB.md) conserva un'origine HTTP per documento, script e `fetch`, con risorsa/redirect esterni bloccati. Il filtro seccomp viene installato prima di avviare Chromium ed è verificato nel suo `/proc`; la prova locale ARM64 passa in container senza rete, con filesystem di sola lettura e quote di memoria/PID. L'[ADR-016](DOCS/it/ADR-016-LINUX-CDP-TRIAL.md) documenta la prima versione con risposte sintetiche interne, senza gate/broker.
- La [revisione ADR-017](DOCS/it/ADR-017-CDP-BROKER-BOUNDARY.md) passa connessioni Unix al proxy già aperte dal genitore: nel laboratorio HTTP documento, script e `fetch` attraversano gate e broker, risorsa fuori scope e redirect esterno sono fermati. Quaranta ripetizioni ARM64 sono passate con 1 GiB/256 PID; 128 PID causavano un errore intermittente all'avvio Chromium. Il laboratorio usa `--no-sandbox` e non chiude il primo criterio M3.
- L'[ADR-019](DOCS/it/ADR-019-CDP-HTTPS-BOUNDARY.md) estende solo il laboratorio Linux CDP a un'origine HTTPS sintetica: il broker verifica il certificato e il nome dell'host con una radice effimera, il proxy Unix accetta richieste HTTPS assolute senza CONNECT e gate, scope, redirect e budget restano applicati. Le fixture HTTP/HTTPS, il caso di nome errato e la negazione sul proxy TCP sono passati localmente; le prove container `none` e `bridge`, la suite Go completa e il race detector mirato sono passati. Il runtime browser di prodotto, sandbox file, revoca nel desktop, GUI e altri OS restano aperti.
- La [prova di revoca CDP](DOCS/it/M3-BROWSER-CDP-LAB.md) cancella una richiesta `fetch` già arrivata al target quando viene revocata la run e nega la richiesta seguente senza un altro contatto target, per entrambe le origini HTTP/HTTPS. Sono passate cinque ripetizioni locali in ciascuna modalità container `none`/`bridge`, più suite Go, race detector mirato, vet, self-test Qt e controlli documentali. Non dimostra revoca nel runtime desktop o contenimento di pagine ostili.
- La [prova negativa di rete CDP](DOCS/it/M3-BROWSER-CDP-LAB.md) usa un listener TCP loopback avviato dal genitore: l'helper filtrato riceve `EPERM` anche con rete `bridge` disponibile, mentre una build diagnostica senza filtro fallisce il controllo. Dieci ripetizioni per ciascuna modalità container (`none`, `bridge`) sono passate localmente. Il canary del renderer non aveva discriminato l'assenza del filtro e non viene usato come evidenza.
- Il [quinto blocco core M3](DOCS/it/M3-SESSIONS.md) aggiunge un POST form di login confermato e vincolato a URL esatto, sessioni effimere per identità/progetto e verifica positiva distinta dalla risposta anonima ([ADR-012](DOCS/it/ADR-012-TEST-SESSIONS.md)). - L'estensione [CSRF M3](DOCS/it/M3-SESSIONS.md) usa solo l'URL di login esatto e già confermato: una GET anonima legge un unico campo hidden dichiarato e, facoltativamente, un cookie host-only di pre-sessione per il POST. ### Problemi aperti

- **M1 è chiusa come alpha tecnica controllata** per decisione dell'autore del 25 settembre 2026: i test locali/CI sostituiscono i precedenti gate umani come criterio di milestone ([matrice IT](DOCS/it/M1-VALIDATION.md)). Le prove H01…H05 e sulle versioni minime reali non sono superate; servono per eventuali dichiarazioni di supporto/accessibilità. Il parere professionale e un CLA attivo restano condizioni per distribuzione/contratti o contributi pertinenti, non gate M1. Mac locale Apple Silicon/macOS 26.6.2 con un display; Windows 10 dell'autore disponibile ma senza prova documentata.
- Il broker pubblico non è stato provato su rete pubblica reale. Archivi separati non condividono un limite globale; manca feedback sul carico del target e un firewall egress indipendente. La policy non interpreta query né garantisce che GET sia senza effetti. La GUI non offre ancora rinnovo/revoca, backup o ripristino guidato.
- La GUI M2 non conserva correlazioni dopo la sessione né offre recupero guidato. La cache e il matching coprono solo il sottoinsieme documentato, senza catalogo completo, test CVE attivi o misura di accuratezza reale. La verifica grafica locale non sostituisce prove assistive e hardware su tutti i target. Browser autenticato nel prodotto, retest e AI restano futuri. Contributi esterni non concedono automaticamente diritti di rilicenza ([CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- La [matrice M3](DOCS/it/M3-VALIDATION.md) lascia aperti tutti e quattro i criteri. ### Technical decisions to preserve

- The 10,000-fixture M0 lab stays separate from scanning. The [technical M1 alpha](DOCS/en/M1-VALIDATION.md) connects an operator-declared/authorized project, managed run, controlled HTTP(S) broker, observational HTML discovery, `HTTP-XCTO-001` check, redacted ledger and IT/EN GUI. This is one initial header check, not a general audit. No LLM is required. The M1 GUI supports one origin and seed per scan; the core supports more within explicit bounds.
- The first [M2 core block](DOCS/en/M2-INTELLIGENCE-CACHE.md) adds a separate SQLite CVE/NVD cache, transactional NVD synchronization for explicit windows and CVE lookup by ID, with hashes/provenance and freshness status. Synthetic local pagination, rollback and reopen tests passed; no real feed sync or full catalog import was performed.
- [M2 core matching](DOCS/en/M2-MATCHING.md) requires explicit product/version signals and keeps `unknown` for unsupported conditions or schemes; it does not infer CVEs from M1 headers. Backports and verification require explicit attestations, not automatic proof. Synthetic fixtures and local regressions passed; no real-corpus accuracy measure exists yet.
- The [extra M2 simulation](DOCS/en/M2-ACCURACY-SIMULATION.md) measures only 30 synthetic cases: 17 correct conclusions among 20 known-truth cases, three abstentions and ten indeterminate cases. NVD without a full CPE now requires the `a`/`o`/`h` part; unproven CVE qualifiers and conflicting versions remain `unknown`. This is not a real-world accuracy measure.
- The [M2 report core](DOCS/en/M2-REPORTS.md) exports a finished run to IT/EN JSON/HTML with a JCS manifest, Ed25519 JWS and offline verification using external trust. Signing uses an operator key in the native credential store; rotation and revocation act on the local public registry. Synthetic tampering and local regression tests passed, but offline trust does not guarantee current revocation or an attested clock.
- The [extended M2 desktop](DOCS/en/M2-GUI-EXTENSION.md) exposes manual NVD/CVE updates, CPE part/full CPE, attested backport and verification, both source states, export/verification and local public key management. Form attestations reset when the signal/CVE/run changes; GUI assessments remain ephemeral until export. The private key stays in the native credential store; Qt regressions with a synthetic key store and Cocoa/offscreen IT/EN rendering were checked locally. Never infer CVEs from M1 headers or download feeds on startup.
- The [first M3 core block](DOCS/en/M3-BROWSER-GATE.md) adds a managed-run browser request gate: scope/path, admitted types, budget, concurrency and revocation. There is no browser adapter yet; workers and WebSockets are denied. An interceptor does not prove egress containment or process limits.
- The [second M3 core block](DOCS/en/M3-BROWSER-PROXY.md) adds an anonymous loopback HTTP proxy with an ephemeral credential, traversing the gate and pinned broker ([ADR-009](DOCS/en/ADR-009-BROWSER-HTTP-BOUNDARY.md)). Synthetic `httptest` cases and local regressions passed; HTTPS CONNECT remains denied. A later optional adapter binds one test session without forwarding browser cookies. The proxy cannot force an external process to use it.
- The [M3 browser lab](DOCS/en/M3-BROWSER-LAB.md) uses Qt WebEngine/MIQT with an ephemeral profile, interceptor and proxy on local fixtures ([ADR-010](DOCS/en/ADR-010-QT-BROWSER-LAB.md)). The local macOS trial passed document, script, `fetch`, and blocked subresource/redirect cases; the lab passes the macOS and Linux CI jobs for commit `b82693a`. This is neither a desktop feature nor proven process isolation.
- The [fourth experimental M3 block](DOCS/en/ADR-011-BROWSER-HELPER.md) moves Qt to a separate helper: configuration and proxy credential over a pipe only after the process boundary, reduced environment and private temporary directory, bounded runtime/output and descendant termination. Synthetic tests and the local Qt lab passed; branch/merge CI must be checked per commit. The Windows Job Object was cross-compiled locally but not executed here; independent egress and aggregate quotas remain absent.
- The [M3 Unix resource block](DOCS/en/M3-BROWSER-RESOURCE-LIMITS.md) applies hard limits in the helper before Qt for open files, file size and core dumps on macOS/Linux. A synthetic test checks inheritance by a descendant and unchanged parent limits; the local macOS Qt lab and Go regressions passed. macOS rejected `RLIMIT_AS`; the first Linux attempt with 16 GiB loaded the document without executing its script. The virtual-address limit was removed because of possible interference with large V8 reservations; the exact cause is unproven. Aggregate memory/process limits and independent egress remain absent.
- The [M3 IPC trial](DOCS/en/M3-BROWSER-UNIX-SCHEME.md) uses an authenticated Unix-socket proxy and a Qt scheme for a synthetic document, script and `fetch` on macOS ([ADR-013](DOCS/en/ADR-013-BROWSER-UNIX-SCHEME.md)). Local proxy tests, the Go suite and Qt lab passed. The scheme does not reproduce HTTP status/headers or the real origin. It is not integrated into the desktop and does not close the browser criterion. The Linux job's Qt 6.4 cannot exercise scheme `fetch`, which requires `FetchApiAllowed` from Qt 6.6.
- The [M3 Linux filter](DOCS/en/M3-BROWSER-LINUX-NETWORK.md) applies seccomp `TSYNC` in the second helper before Qt. The initial version denied new INET sockets; the [ADR-014 revision](DOCS/en/ADR-014-LINUX-BROWSER-NETWORK.md) passes four parent-connected broker descriptors and also denies new Unix `connect` calls, datagram sockets and destination-bearing `sendto`, preserving Qt IPC on connected sockets. A Linux ARM64 container test, local Linux Qt lab and its CI job pass; the first HTTP helper remains unfiltered and the browser is not in the desktop. Aggregate quotas, HTTP(S) semantics and other platforms remain open.
- [M3 platform feasibility](DOCS/en/M3-BROWSER-PLATFORM-FEASIBILITY.md) records temporary local trials: a network-denied App Sandbox bundle blocks TCP; Qt WebEngine 6.11.2 fails at Chromium Mach registration even with Chromium sandbox disabled; WebKit with a synthetic scheme passes without App Sandbox and with outbound permission, but does not load the page in a bundle without it. These are local observations, not proof of general impossibility. Windows Qt WebEngine does not compile with MinGW. [ADR-015](DOCS/en/ADR-015-BROWSER-PLATFORM-BOUNDARY.md) proposes keeping the browser out of the GUI and the first M3 item open until an OS boundary and runtime are verified on each platform; it does not change the author's Go/Qt Widgets desktop choice.
- The [Linux Chromium/CDP trial](DOCS/en/M3-BROWSER-CDP-LAB.md) preserves an HTTP origin for document, script and `fetch`, with outside resource/redirect requests blocked. The seccomp filter is installed before Chromium starts and checked in its `/proc`; the local ARM64 trial passes in a networkless container with a read-only filesystem and memory/PID quotas. [ADR-016](DOCS/en/ADR-016-LINUX-CDP-TRIAL.md) records the first version with hard-coded responses and no gate/broker.
- The [ADR-017 revision](DOCS/en/ADR-017-CDP-BROKER-BOUNDARY.md) passes already connected Unix proxy descriptors from the parent: document, script and `fetch` cross the gate and broker in the HTTP lab, while an outside resource and redirect are stopped. Forty ARM64 repetitions passed with 1 GiB/256 PIDs; 128 PIDs caused an intermittent Chromium startup failure. The lab uses `--no-sandbox` and does not close the first M3 item.
- [ADR-019](DOCS/en/ADR-019-CDP-HTTPS-BOUNDARY.md) extends only the Linux CDP lab to a synthetic HTTPS origin: the broker verifies the certificate and hostname with an ephemeral root, the Unix proxy accepts absolute HTTPS requests without CONNECT, and gate, scope, redirect and budget still apply. HTTP/HTTPS fixtures, wrong-hostname rejection and TCP proxy denial passed locally; container trials with `none` and `bridge` networking, the full Go suite and targeted race detector passed. The product browser runtime, file sandbox, desktop revocation, GUI and other OSs remain open.
- The [CDP revocation trial](DOCS/en/M3-BROWSER-CDP-LAB.md) cancels a `fetch` already at the target when the run is revoked and denies the next request without another target contact, for both HTTP and HTTPS origins. Five local repetitions passed in each `none`/`bridge` container mode, together with the Go suite, targeted race detector, vet, Qt self-test and documentation checks. It does not prove revocation in a desktop runtime or containment of hostile pages.
- The [CDP network negative trial](DOCS/en/M3-BROWSER-CDP-LAB.md) uses a loopback TCP listener started by the parent: the filtered helper receives `EPERM` even with `bridge` networking available, while a diagnostic build without the filter fails the check. Ten local repetitions passed in each container mode (`none`, `bridge`). A renderer canary did not distinguish the filter's absence and is not used as evidence.
- The [fifth M3 core block](DOCS/en/M3-SESSIONS.md) adds a confirmed form POST to one exact login URL, ephemeral sessions per identity/project and positive verification distinct from the anonymous response ([ADR-012](DOCS/en/ADR-012-TEST-SESSIONS.md)). Local fixtures, race-detector regressions and branch/merge CI passed (merge `7159807`). The first core constructor was loopback-only; a bounded two-account desktop workflow uses it, while the Qt helper remains separate.
- The [M3 CSRF extension](DOCS/en/M3-SESSIONS.md) uses only the exact, already confirmed login URL: an anonymous GET reads one declared hidden field and optionally a host-only pre-session cookie for POST. The authenticated cookie must be new, and per-identity verification remains mandatory. Two-identity fixtures, negative cases, race detector, Go suite and IT/EN Qt self-test passed locally. Other CSRF flows, MFA/OIDC, bearer tokens, continuous rotation during confirmation or changes of name/path, and complete trials on authorized public staging remain open.
- [M3 cookie rotation](DOCS/en/M3-SESSIONS.md) keeps one host-only session with the same name/path and verifies the same identity marker before replacing its value. Session requests are serialized; the extra confirmation consumes budget and does not extend the deadline. A second rotation during confirmation or an ambiguous response closes the session. Two identities, cross-role check, proxy, concurrency and negative fixtures passed on loopback with the race detector; local Go suite and IT/EN Qt self-test passed. Complete public staging trials, MFA/OIDC, bearer tokens and continuous rotation remain open.
- The [M3 authenticated public transport](DOCS/en/ADR-018-AUTHENTICATED-PUBLIC-TRANSPORT.md) is a separate core constructor: one HTTPS grant with pinned public IPs, separate confirmation, at most 128 requests/15 minutes and at least 500 ms between requests. It contacted no Internet target; the core two-account run can select the public grant.
- The [core public two-account run](DOCS/en/M3-CROSS-ROLE-RUN.md) explicitly selects the HTTPS broker with `CrossRoleRunPinnedPublic` and also requires `PublicConfirmed`; the desktop dialog can now select it with separate confirmation. Invalid mode, confirmation and IPs are rejected before networking; denied synthetic DNS yields inconclusive without a socket. The Go suite, targeted race detector, vet and local Qt self-test passed. Broker TLS and loopback two-account trials are separate; a complete trial on authorized public staging remains open.
- The [M3 public dialog](DOCS/en/M3-AUTH-DESKTOP.md) shows the project’s exact origin and requires a fourth confirmation for credentials on an HTTPS grant; selected budget at most 128, minimum 500 ms pacing, 64 KiB body and zero redirects. Excessive budget or changed origin is rejected before a run. No public target was contacted; a complete authorized staging trial remains open.
- The [sixth M3 core block](DOCS/en/M3-OPENAPI-IMPORT.md) imports OpenAPI 3.0/3.1 JSON offline into an ephemeral inventory, with a separately declared origin, parser limits, no resolved `$ref` and static GET/HEAD candidates under scope/policy. Local trials and branch/merge CI passed (merge `359d382`). - Require declared consent, exact origin, GET/path policy, exact IP grants, budgets and pacing; follow links only with opt-in and never submit discovered forms automatically. M3 login POST needs a separately confirmed URL. Public mode needs manually pinned public IPs ([ADR-008 EN](DOCS/en/ADR-008-PINNED-PUBLIC-TRANSPORT.md)); tests contacted no external target. - SQLite **v4** stores declarations/revisions and only run/visit codes and counts, without visited URLs, queries, headers, bodies or credentials ([store EN](DOCS/en/M1-PROJECT-STORE.md)). The macOS bundle applies the Qt Cocoa correction ([ADR-006 EN](DOCS/en/ADR-006-QT-COCOA.md)); Windows CI uses the MSYS2 package lock ([EN guide](DOCS/en/DEVELOPMENT.md)).
- Do not commit secrets, private keys, client data, weights, CVE databases or real reports. Do not scan external systems without targets and activities explicitly authorized in the request.

### Open problems

- **M1 is closed as a controlled technical alpha** by the author's September 25, 2026 decision: local/CI tests replace the earlier human gates as milestone criteria ([EN matrix](DOCS/en/M1-VALIDATION.md)). H01…H05 and real minimum-version trials have not passed; they remain necessary for any corresponding support/accessibility claims. Professional advice and an active CLA remain conditions for relevant distribution/contracts or contributions, not M1 gates. Local Mac: Apple Silicon/macOS 26.6.2 with one display; the author's Windows 10 PC is available but has no documented trial.
- Public transport has not been tested on a real public network. Separate stores do not share an overall limit; target load feedback and independent egress firewall are missing. Policy does not interpret queries or guarantee that GET has no effects. The GUI does not yet offer authorization renewal/revocation, backup or guided restore.
- The M2 GUI does not persist assessments across sessions or offer guided recovery. The cache and matching cover only the documented subset, without a full catalog, active CVE checks or a real-world accuracy measure. Local visual checks do not replace assistive and hardware trials on every target. Authenticated browsing in the product, retests and AI remain future work. External contributions do not automatically grant relicensing rights ([CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- The [M3 status matrix](DOCS/en/M3-VALIDATION.md) leaves all four criteria open. M3 still needs desktop integration of the browser runtime, independent egress with pinned IPs, hard macOS/Linux process limits, a Windows Qt trial and complete SPA/API fixtures before the roadmap's first criterion can be considered complete. The cross-role check remains a narrow first case; a persistent identity and resource inventory is missing.
- The anonymous M3 proxy admits HTTP GET/HEAD without cookies; the CDP lab now separately tests synthetic HTTPS. Session mode uses parent-side cookies only for loopback GETs, but CSRF flows beyond one hidden field, MFA/OIDC login, continuous rotation during confirmation, HTTPS in the product browser, persistent account configuration and complete public staging trials are absent: login SPAs are not yet covered. Negative trials with a real browser on each platform are needed before desktop enablement.
- The Qt lab uses a supervised separate helper with the narrow Unix limits above; the revised Linux filter denies new Unix connections and INET sockets on the experimental scheme path. General proof of the OS boundary, aggregate process/memory quotas, HTTPS mediation in the product runtime and safe macOS/Windows runtimes remain absent; Qt WebEngine packaging/license review is open.
