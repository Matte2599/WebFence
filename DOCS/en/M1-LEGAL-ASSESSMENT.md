# M1 — Internal legal assessment of license and contributions

[Italiano](../it/M1-LEGAL-ASSESSMENT.md) · [Dossier](LEGAL-REVIEW.md) · [License](../../LICENSE) · [Governance](LICENSING.md)

**Subject:** WebFence Community License 1.0, SHA-256 `d987f614fd24db59be011b42366c5054437e59af3cba6840612f844b39c2aef8`, and [CONTRIBUTING](../../CONTRIBUTING.md), reviewed on September 25, 2026. This is an internal assessment of the text and cited sources; **it is not an independent lawyer's opinion, a finding of enforceability in every jurisdiction, or approval of packages for distribution**. The author's decision closes M1 using this assessment without claiming that professional review was obtained.

## Reasoned findings

| Topic | Internal assessment | Action before relevant use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intent and classification | Sections 2–5 distinguish personal/independent professional use, paid engagements with report delivery, company use and software distribution. Royalties do not arise automatically. The company-use restriction makes **source-available**, rather than “open source” under the [OSI definition](https://opensource.org/osd), the correct description. | Keep that name and the scenario table in [LICENSING](LICENSING.md); do not use OSI badges. |
| Parties and acceptance | “Independent Professional”, sole proprietor, contractor and Company are defined, but mixed arrangements, client-owned devices, foreign entities and acceptance mechanics can be disputed. The license specifies no governing law, venue or method of proving assent. The [EU Software Directive 2009/24/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32009L0024) preserves exceptions and lawful-acquirer rights; their application cannot be settled by the repository text alone. | Define parties, capacity, territory, copies/users, acceptance and governing law for company agreements with qualified counsel. Do not promise universal enforceability. |
| Forks, versions and reports | Section 3 permits public forks and hosting, but limits recipients' operational rights; hosting terms may grant separate rights. Section 6 permits delivery of report-template content without transferring the program. Section 11 protects rights already granted: later license changes are not retroactive. | Version and publish any license change; do not silently alter 1.0. |
| Qt and other dependencies | Section 1 does not relicense third-party components. Qt LGPL use with a non-free application is possible in principle **only if all applicable duties are met**, including corresponding source, notices and the ability to replace/run modified libraries; some modules are GPL-only. [Qt's official guidance](https://www.qt.io/development/open-source-lgpl-obligations) explains this. WebFence inventories are technical evidence, not package-compliance certification. | Review the exact components in an artifact and each license before distribution; do not apply WebFence restrictions to Qt rights. |
| Contributors and dual licensing | Section 8 leaves copyright with contributors and gives the author no additional commercial rights. Publishing a PR or signing a DCO does not replace an inbound agreement. | Until a versioned contributor agreement is accepted by the individual or corporate rights holder, do not merge substantial contributions intended for commercial relicensing; see [CONTRIBUTING](../../CONTRIBUTING.md). |
| Warranty and security assessments | Sections 7, 9, 10 and 12 distinguish target authorization, warranty exclusions and non-waivable rights. Liability terms vary by law and user type; a signed report does not certify security. | Review commercial contracts and report terms separately. Do not present the M1 scan as a complete audit. |

**M1 decision:** no unilateral change to `LICENSE` is needed for the local alpha; the internal reading found no obvious contradiction with the author's requirements. **Still open as conditions before relevant distribution/contracts or contributions, rather than M1 gates:** professional advice on jurisdictions and actual packages, component-specific third-party compliance, and a contributor agreement with an acceptance process. There is no active CLA or standard commercial grant. The [`LicenseRef-` identifier](https://spdx.github.io/spdx-spec/v2.3/using-SPDX-short-identifiers-in-source-files/) is a local reference to a custom license, not SPDX/OSI approval.
